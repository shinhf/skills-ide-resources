# Pedagogy Sources

> **MENTOR-SIDE REFERENCE.** This file is here so *you* can tell what the evidence actually
> supports before repeating a number. Do not hand it to a trainee — a trainee who is shown the
> research on being questioned starts evaluating the method instead of the problem, and the
> effect sizes below are not strong enough to survive that scrutiny mid-dialogue.

Every claim carries a strength tag. Use the tag, not the headline.

- **[STRONG]** — replicated, large samples, consistent direction.
- **[MODERATE]** — real but modest, or replicated with caveats.
- **[CONTESTED]** — a direct challenge exists in the literature and is not resolved.
- **[CONVENTIONAL]** — widely taught, weakly evidenced; useful as vocabulary.

---

## Two honesty rules

These come first because they are the two claims this plugin would otherwise make by default, and both are wrong.

### 1. Do not cite Bloom's "2 sigma"

**[CONTESTED — and the popular figure is not defensible.]** The two-sigma claim rested on two dissertations using narrow, experimenter-made tests. Of 96 tutoring studies subsequently reviewed, **none reproduced a two-sigma effect**, and the average was **0.37 SD**. Real-world private tutoring for disadvantaged students has come in at **0.06 SD** ([Education Next audit](https://www.educationnext.org/two-sigma-tutoring-separating-science-fiction-from-science-fact/), [Nintil](https://nintil.com/bloom-sigma/)).

Cite **~0.4 SD** if citing anything at all. Note the accompanying pattern: tutoring studies with fewer than 100 participants average d ≈ 0.38, while larger samples give d ≈ 0.11 — the effect shrinks as the sample grows, which is the signature of small-study bias rather than a robust intervention.

### 2. Do not claim conversation beats good explanation

**[STRONG, and it cuts against the intuition.]** VanLehn's interaction plateau: step-based tutoring reached d ≈ 0.76 and was **statistically indistinguishable** from human tutoring at d ≈ 0.79, and going finer than step granularity — sub-step conversational elaboration — bought **no additional measured learning** ([VanLehn 2011](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00461520.2011.611369)).

So the justification for running these commands as conversations is **diagnosis, engagement and learner ownership — not a proven learning-gain multiplier.** State it in those terms whenever the method is questioned. A dialogue that produces the same document as a good explanation has bought a better diagnosis of where the trainee is, and nothing more; that is enough reason, and it is the only reason available.

## Frameworks used as vocabulary

**ICAP (Chi & Wylie 2014)** — **[CONTESTED]**. The Interactive > Constructive > Active > Passive ordering is the design vocabulary this plugin borrows when it demands that a turn make the trainee *generate* something ([Chi & Wylie 2014](https://education.asu.edu/sites/g/files/litvpz656/files/lcl/chiwylie2014icap_2.pdf)). A 2023 *npj Science of Learning* paper found the **Interactive-vs-Constructive difference negligible and non-significant** ([challenge](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41539-023-00197-4)). Use ICAP as design vocabulary, not as proof that dialogue outranks self-explanation.

**Zone of proximal development** — **[CONVENTIONAL]**. As usually stated it is unfalsifiable: there is no independent measure of the zone. It is operationalisable only as **dynamic assessment** — test, prompt, retest — which is exactly what the hint ladder does ([ZPD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zone_of_proximal_development)). Keep the practice; do not cite the construct as evidence.

**Contingent shift (Wood, Wood & Middleton)** — **[MODERATE]**. One rung down on failure, one rung up on success is the rule the hint ladder implements. Caveat worth remembering: the original experiment had **n = 8 per cell** ([critique](https://www.cognitivepsychology.com/Scaffolding)).

**OARS / motivational interviewing** — **[MODERATE in clinical settings, weak outside]**. g ≈ 0.18 in a school meta-analysis with a prediction interval crossing zero ([meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39645344/), [MI overview](https://motivationalinterviewing.org/understanding-motivational-interviewing)). Borrowed as phrasing technique only.

**Padesky's four stages** — **[CONVENTIONAL]**. A well-specified clinical procedure, adopted here for its structure and its condition that the questioner must not already hold the answer it wants ([Padesky](https://padesky.com/newpad/wp-content/uploads/2012/11/socquest.pdf)).

## Effects that carry real weight

**Retrieval practice** — **[STRONG]**. g ≈ 0.50–0.61 across a large literature; among the best-supported interventions available ([Roediger & Karpicke](http://psychnet.wustl.edu/memory/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/Roediger-Karpicke-2006_PPS.pdf)). This is why the retrieval-check move earns turns that could have gone to explanation.

**Worked-example effect and expertise reversal** — **[STRONG]**. Worked examples help novices and the advantage **reverses** as competence grows ([expertise reversal](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11251-009-9102-0)). Rule 7 is this effect applied: withholding a worked example from someone with no schema is not restraint, it is the wrong dial setting.

**Wait time (Rowe)** — **[STRONG in classrooms]**. Extending the post-question pause to ≥3 seconds produced answers 300–700% longer, with more inference and more learner-generated questions ([Rowe 1986](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/002248718603700110)). **Flag the extrapolation honestly:** there is no pause in a text chat. "One question per turn, nothing after the question mark" is this plugin's structural analogue, and the transfer is an argument, not a finding.

**Productive failure** — **[MODERATE]**. g = 0.36 [0.20, 0.51] across 53 studies for problem-solving before instruction, **conditional on a consolidation phase** ([meta-analysis](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/00346543211019105)). Moderator: the effect **reverses toward instruction-first for young children**. For an adult trainee on a school task the direction holds.

**Elaborative interrogation and self-explanation** — **[MODERATE]**. Both rated only **moderate** utility by Dunlosky et al. 2013, which rated practice testing and distributed practice high ([Dunlosky et al. 2013](https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/spaced-repetition/2013-dunlosky.pdf)). Worth a turn each, not worth building the method on.

**Content-free prompting (Chi et al. 2001)** — **[MODERATE]**. Tutors restricted to content-free prompts — "Could you clarify?", "Why?", "How?" — produced students who learned **"just as effectively"** as those given explanations and feedback ([Chi et al. 2001](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1207/s15516709cog2504_1)). Read the claim as stated: **no worse**, not better. It licenses rung 1; it does not prove rung 1 is superior.

**Question quality vs frequency (Graesser & Person)** — **[MODERATE]**. Question **quality** correlates with achievement; question **frequency** does not ([Graesser & Person](https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/spaced-repetition/1994-graesser.pdf)). Never treat a trainee's question count as a proxy for engagement.

**Information gap and curiosity** — **[MODERATE]**. Curiosity peaks at a nearly-closable gap and collapses when the gap is too large or too small, which is the case for a priming dose of context before the question ([information gap](https://journalofcognition.org/articles/10.5334/joc.501)).

**Autonomy support** — **[MODERATE]**. Providing a rationale for constraints, offering choice, and acknowledging negative affect predict engagement and persistence ([autonomy support](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0361476X23000899)).

**The protégé effect** — **[MODERATE]**. Preparing to teach drives synthesis, which is the warrant for rule 10. Caveat: at least one study found **learner-generated explanation equivalent in the absence of a peer audience**, so the effect may be explanation-generation rather than the audience.

## LLM tutors specifically

**OpenAI Study Mode** — **[CONVENTIONAL — a published design, not a study]**. Its system rules include **one question per step**, **wait for the response before continuing**, and **two attempts before revealing** ([leaked/published prompt](https://gist.github.com/idcesares/c28c7a7189726dc3d8a89b6db92c1c8d)). Rules 1 and 7 of this skill converge on the same numbers independently; treat that as corroboration of practice, not as evidence.

**Google LearnLM** — **[CONVENTIONAL]**. Its stated pedagogical principles explicitly **penalise "excessive information withholding"** alongside rewarding cognitive activation ([LearnLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.16429v1)). This is the clearest external statement of `## The refusal`: withholding is a failure mode with a name, not a safe default.

**Khanmigo, multi-school** — **[MODERATE, and the most important practical finding here]**. A Socratic AI tutor that refused direct answers and offered questions instead saw students **largely abandon it**, with **no measurable benefit** over the non-AI baseline ([Hechinger Report](https://hechingerreport.org/proof-points-khanmigo-math-ai-tutor/)). The cost of conflating "no solutions" with "no teaching" is that the tool goes unused.

**Scaffolding mismatch in LLM tutors** — **[CONTESTED]**. Reported tendency of LLM tutors to scaffold at the wrong grain — hinting where teaching was needed and teaching where a hint would have done ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15766v1)). The explicit ladder plus rule 7 exists to make the grain choice visible rather than improvised.

## Verification gaps

State these when precision matters, and close them before quoting the figures as exact.

- **VanLehn 2011** full text is **paywalled**. The d ≈ 0.76 / d ≈ 0.79 figures here come from secondary summaries and should be re-verified against the published article before being repeated as precise numbers.
- **Chi et al. 2001** full text is **paywalled**. The suppressed-explanation / content-free-prompt result likewise comes from secondary summaries and needs the same re-verification.

Treat both as directionally reliable and numerically provisional. Repeating a paywalled effect size to a decimal place, from a summary, is the same error this file's first honesty rule exists to prevent.
